Privacy Policy

Menitgaming Uploader · last updated 18 October 2026 ·
Applies to the internal tool for @menitgaming

1. What this tool does

Menitgaming Uploader is a private, internal tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel @menitgaming to publish video content owned by that channel.

2. Data we access

Google OAuth is used with a single scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. The tool can only upload videos to the channel that authorizes it. It cannot read analytics, comments, viewer data, or any other Google account data.

3. Data we store

  • An OAuth refresh token issued by Google, stored locally on the operator's own computer (file permissions 600).
  • The video files the operator chooses to upload, until they are uploaded.

No viewer data, analytics, personal data, or third-party data is collected, stored, shared, or sold.

4. Data sharing

No data is transmitted to any third party. There is no advertising, profiling, or tracking. The only outbound requests are to Google's own APIs.

5. Retention and deletion

The stored token is deleted when the tool is removed or access is revoked. Videos are retained on the channel until the operator deletes them.

6. Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revocation immediately invalidates the stored token.

7. Children's privacy

The tool is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

8. Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
