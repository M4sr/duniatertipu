DuniaTertipu Uploader

Internal publishing tool for the YouTube channel @duniatertipu.

What this tool is

A private, single-operator utility that uploads video files owned by the channel operator to the channel itself. It is not a service, has no users other than the channel owner, and is not distributed.

Google API usage

It requests exactly two OAuth scopes: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload (upload videos) and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl (add the operator's own uploads to the operator's own playlists, and delete the operator's own draft videos and playlists). It cannot access any other channel or any other Google account.
